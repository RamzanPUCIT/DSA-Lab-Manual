## 📖 About

This repository contains the official **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) lab manual** used across all programs offering DSA in the Department of Computer Science, University of Okara.

The series is designed with the **2026–2030 industry and research landscape** in mind. Each topic begins with *why it matters* (daily life, industry, research and the AI era), builds the concept step by step with diagrams, pseudocode and dry runs, and ends with hands-on tasks and real interview problems.

- ✅ **Beginner-friendly:** starts from absolute zero; no prior programming required
- ✅ **Concept-first:** language-independent explanations, Python implementations, plus a **C++ Corner**
- ✅ **Hands-on:** every lab has a Colab notebook with **auto-check test cells** (✅ when your answer is correct)
- ✅ **Career-ready:** curated **LeetCode** problems, viva questions and a capstone project

## 🚀 Getting Started (Students)

1. **Read** the lab manual (PDF) before coming to the lab.
2. Click **Open in Colab** for your lab, then choose **File → Save a copy in Drive**.
3. **Run** the examples with `Shift + Enter` and change values to experiment.
4. **Complete** every `# TODO`. Write pseudocode first, then code.
5. Run the **test cell** after each task. ✅ means your solution is correct.
6. **Push** your completed notebook to your own GitHub repository `DSA-Labs/LabNN/`.

> 💡 Notebooks also run locally in **Jupyter Notebook** or **VS Code** (Python 3).

## 👩‍🏫 For Instructors

## 📌 Lab Rules

- Bring your own laptop with Colab or Jupyter working.
- Arrive on time; late arrival may result in no attendance for the lab.
- Copying another student’s code or pasting AI output without understanding it will be awarded **zero**. You must be able to explain every line in the viva.
